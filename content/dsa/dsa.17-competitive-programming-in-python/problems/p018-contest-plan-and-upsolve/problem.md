# Contest plan and upsolve

Your first live contest is a 2-hour round with six problems (A to F, roughly in increasing difficulty). Write, in `answer.md`:

1. A **plan of 5 lines or more** for the two hours: what you do in the first minutes, how you spend your time, what you do when you are stuck, what you do in the last minutes.
2. A short **upsolving routine** for the day after the contest: what you do for a problem you did not solve, and for one you solved slowly.

Then compare with the model answer and tick the checklist. Be concrete: give times and rules, not wishes ("be calm").

Your answer should answer these questions:

- What do you do in the first 5 to 10 minutes?
- How long may you stay on one problem before you switch? What triggers the switch?
- Which problems do you read first, and what do you do with the ones everybody solves quickly?
- How do you avoid wrong submissions?
- What do you do in the last 10 minutes?
- What do you record, and what do you do afterwards?
