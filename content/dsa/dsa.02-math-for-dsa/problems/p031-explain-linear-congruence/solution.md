## What a good answer contains

- Solvable if and only if `gcd(a, m)` divides `b`.
- Exactly `g = gcd(a, m)` solutions modulo `m`, spaced `m / g` apart.
- The reduction to `a' x = b' (mod m')` with `gcd(a', m') = 1`, then one inverse.
- The special cases and the link to the modular inverse.

## Rate yourself

- 3 points: the solvability condition with both checks (6x=4 and 4x=3).
- 3 points: the count `g` and the spacing `m / g`.
- 2 points: the solving steps with the worked example.
- 2 points: special cases and the inverse link.

8 or more means you are ready for the solver problem.
