## Hint 1

Write the table of `4 * x % 10` for `x = 0..9`. Which remainders can you get? What do they have in common with 10?

## Hint 2

If `d` divides both `a` and `m`, what must be true for `a * x - k * m`? Can that ever equal 1?

## Hint 3

Fermat's little theorem has a condition on the modulus. Try `a = 2`, `m = 9` and compute `2^7 % 9`, then compare with the
real inverse (find it by trying `x = 1..8`).
