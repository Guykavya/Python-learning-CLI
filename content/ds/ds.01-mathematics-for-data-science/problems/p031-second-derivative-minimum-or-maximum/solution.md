## Approach

The estimates are `2`, `-2`, `0` and `2e-6`; only the first two leave the band `[-1e-4, 1e-4]`.

## Answer

```text
x^2 at 0: minimum
-x^2 at 0: maximum
x^3 at 0: flat
x^4 at 0: flat
```

## Another way

`x^4` has a true minimum at 0 although its second derivative is 0 there: you need the fourth derivative or a look at nearby points.
