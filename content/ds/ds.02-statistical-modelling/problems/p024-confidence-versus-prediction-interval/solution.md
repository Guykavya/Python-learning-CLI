## Walk-through

Both intervals are centred on the fitted value at x = 1000. The confidence interval adds only the uncertainty about where the line is. The prediction interval also adds the scatter of single flats around the line. More data pin the line down, but they do not make single flats less variable.

## Check with the numbers

Half-width of the prediction interval at n = 10,000 is about `(42.78 - 27.28) / 2 = 7.75`, which is almost `1.96 * 4 = 7.84`: it is the noise itself.
