## Hint 1

Start with what question each number answers: how much is explained? is the whole model better than a flat line? how big is the effect? how uncertain? how many standard errors from zero? how surprising under no effect?

## Hint 2

For the last two lines of the summary: Durbin-Watson looks at neighbouring residuals (values near 2 mean no correlation); Jarque-Bera looks at skew and kurtosis (a large p-value means no evidence against normal residuals).
