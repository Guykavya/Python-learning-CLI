# Row and column totals

Read `r c`, then `r` lines of `c` integers. Print each row followed by ` = ` and its sum. Then
print the column sums after `Columns:` and the grand total after `Total:`.

**Example**

Input:
```
2 3
1 2 3
4 5 6
```
Output:
```
1 2 3 = 6
4 5 6 = 15
Columns: 5 7 9
Total: 21
```
