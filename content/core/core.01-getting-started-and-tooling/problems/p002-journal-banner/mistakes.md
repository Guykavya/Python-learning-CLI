- Typing the border by hand and getting 29 or 31 signs. The test reports the
  length so you can see it.
- Losing the leading spaces: `print("PYTHON LEARNING JOURNAL")`. Spaces inside
  quotes are part of the text.
- Writing `print(" ", "PYTHON ...")`. `print` adds its own separator space, so
  you get a different number of spaces than you expect.
