- Renaming a variable in one place but not where it is used: `NameError`.
- `total_Marks` or `Total_marks`: still has a capital letter.
- Removing the spaces in keyword arguments is right (`sep="-"`), but removing
  them around assignment `=` is not. The two `=` follow different rules.
- Renaming `l` to `L`. It is still one letter and now looks like a constant.
