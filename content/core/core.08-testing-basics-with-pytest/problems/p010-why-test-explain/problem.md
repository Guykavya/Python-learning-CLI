# Explain: why write tests?

Asha has written `final_price(price, discount_percent)` and tried it twice in the REPL. It worked,
so she says "tests are a waste of time". Her friend Ravi says "I run my program by hand after every
change, that is my testing".

In `answer.md` (about 120 to 250 words), explain:

1. What is an automated test, in one or two sentences? Use the words **input**, **expected
   output** and **assert**.
2. Give **three** concrete reasons why Asha's two REPL tries are not enough, and why Ravi's hand
   testing does not scale. Use the word **regression** (a change that breaks something that
   used to work).
3. What does a test cost, and why is that cost usually worth it?
4. A test only checks cases you thought of. Name one thing a passing test suite does **not** prove.
