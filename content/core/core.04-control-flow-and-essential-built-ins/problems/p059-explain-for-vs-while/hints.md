## Hint 1

List what can go wrong in the `while` version: where `i` starts, the comparison, the
increment. Which of these exist in `for ch in word:`?

## Hint 2

`while` fits when you cannot know the number of iterations before starting. Think about
reading input until a word, or a value that changes until a condition is met.

How many numbers does `range(a, b)` give? How does `range(len(s))` relate to valid positions
in `s`?
