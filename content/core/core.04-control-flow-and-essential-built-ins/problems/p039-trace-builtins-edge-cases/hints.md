## Hint 1

`any` of nothing is `False`; `all` of nothing is `True`. `[0]` is a non-empty list.

## Hint 2

Capital letters come before small letters in plain string comparison. With `key=`, the
comparison uses the key result, but the **original item** is returned.

## Hint 3

When two items compare equal, `min` and `max` return the first one. `4 == 4.0` is `True`.
