# When RFM misleads

Chapter section 5.7. RFM is a simple, useful tool, and it can fail quietly. In `answer.md`, for each of the three businesses below say **what goes wrong** with RFM scores, and **what you would do instead**. About 250 to 350 words.

1. **A gift shop** that sells 60% of its yearly revenue in the three weeks before December 25.
2. **A streaming service** with a monthly subscription: every active customer pays once a month, and customers cancel with one click.
3. **A B2B supplier** where one customer placed a single order of 4 million rupees and everyone else orders between 5,000 and 50,000.
