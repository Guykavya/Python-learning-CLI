# Segments: look, do not decide

Chapter section 2.10. After a test with no overall winner, an analyst slices it into **8 segments** (device, city, new vs returning, and so on) and finds that "mobile users in Large cities" show
a significant lift of +2.1 points (p = 0.03). The team wants to roll the banner out to that segment.

In `answer.md`, in about 150 to 250 words:

1. explain why this is **not** a result yet (use the numbers: 8 segments, 5% level);
2. say what else makes a segment finding unreliable (think of how many visitors it has);
3. say what the team should do instead.

## Example

A start: "If the banner does nothing at all and we test eight segments at the 5% level, the chance that at least one of them looks significant is about 34%..."
