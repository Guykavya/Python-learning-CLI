# When the median beats the mean

Chapter section 10. A town council has the monthly household incomes (in rupees) of ten households:

```text
18000  20000  21000  22000  24000  25000  27000  30000  32000  600000
```

The last household is a business owner. A councillor says: "The average household earns 81,900 rupees a month, so our town is rich and needs no support scheme."

In `answer.md` write about 200 to 350 words:

1. Compute the **mean** and the **median** and say which is which.
2. Explain which one you would report to describe a **typical household**, and why.
3. Explain what the large gap between them tells you about the data.
4. Say when the mean is still the right number to use.
5. Say what you would report if you had to give one summary line to the council.

Use the words *skew*, *outlier*, *typical household*, *share* and *median*.

## Example

"The mean is the total divided by the number of households, so a single very large value pulls it up. It says little about what most households have."
