## Hint 1

For each list write mean, median and the squared deviations from the mean. Divide their sum by 5 and take the square root to get `pstdev`. Then `gap = (mean - median) / pstdev`.

## Hint 2

For `[100, 101, 102, 103, 110]` the mean is 103.2 and the median 102, so the difference is 1.2. Compare 1.2 with 10% of 102 (that is 10.2) for the verdict, and with the standard deviation (about 3.5) for the gap.
