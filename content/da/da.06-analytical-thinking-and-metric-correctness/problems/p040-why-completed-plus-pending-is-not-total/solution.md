## Approach

The model answer in `answer.md` goes question by question. The principle behind it: a total equals the sum of its parts
only when the parts are a **partition** of the same population: one date basis, and every status in exactly one part.
Here the total is a cohort (by creation), and the parts are an activity view (by start), with two statuses missing.

## Complexity

Nothing to compute. The chapter's bridge for February is: 895, -87 (started later), -84 (cancelled), -13 (failed),
+98 (created earlier, started in February), = 809.
