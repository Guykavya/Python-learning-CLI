## Approach

Case 1: zero correlation, perfect dependence. Case 2: one point drags Pearson to 0.8 while Spearman, which sees only order, stays at 1.000. Case 3: a perfect increasing curve gives Pearson 0.933 but Spearman 1.000.

## Check

Run the program and compare.

## Another way

Always plot the data. r near 0 does not mean "no relationship", and r below 1 does not mean "not a clean relationship": it only measures the straight-line part.
