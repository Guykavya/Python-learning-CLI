## Hint 1

Compute `0.95^k` for k = 1, 5, 20, 100 (0.95, 0.774, 0.358, 0.0059) and subtract it from 1. Then divide 0.05 by k.

## Hint 2

```text
k=1   1 - 0.95   = 0.050 ; 0.05/1   = 0.0500
k=5   1 - 0.7738 = 0.226 ; 0.05/5   = 0.0100
k=20  1 - 0.3585 = 0.642 ; 0.05/20  = 0.0025
k=100 1 - 0.0059 = 0.994 ; 0.05/100 = 0.0005
:3d pads k to 3 characters
```
