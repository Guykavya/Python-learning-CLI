# A narrow interval around the wrong value

Chapter section 4.7. A company wants to know the average monthly spend of its customers. Two sources are available:

- an **opt-in** pop-up on the app that 200,000 customers answered, giving mean 2,950 rupees with a 95% interval of 2,940 to 2,960;
- a **random sample** of 400 customers, contacted until almost all answered, giving mean 2,500 with a 95% interval of 2,420 to 2,580.

The manager says: "The first interval is tiny; use it."

In `answer.md`, in about 150 to 250 words:

1. Which estimate would you trust, and why?
2. What exactly does a confidence interval measure, and what does it **not** measure?
3. What happens to the biased estimate as the number of responses grows?
4. What would you do to improve the first source, and what would you not do?

## Example

A start: "A confidence interval measures sampling error only: how much the estimate would change if the same procedure were repeated.
It says nothing about whether the procedure is aimed at the right population..."
