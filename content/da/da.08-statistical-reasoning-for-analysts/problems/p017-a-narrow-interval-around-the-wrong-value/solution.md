## Approach

The point is the difference between precision and accuracy (problem 5): sample size controls precision, the sampling method controls bias.

## Code

Nothing to run. A simulation makes it visible: draw biased samples of growing size from a population and watch the interval shrink around a value that is not the population mean.

## Another way

If the opt-in data are all you have, report them as "app users who chose to answer" and weight them (problem 7) with the known shares of segments, with a warning that weighting cannot fix selection on unmeasured traits.
