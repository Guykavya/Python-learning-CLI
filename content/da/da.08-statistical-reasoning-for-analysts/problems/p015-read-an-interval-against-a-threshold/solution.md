## Approach

Strict inequalities mean that an interval touching the MEI is inconclusive. Formatting: `{lo:5.2f}` pads positive numbers with one leading space (`" 0.60"`) and leaves `"-0.40"` as is.

## Check

Run the program and compare.

## Another way

Decide in advance what an end exactly on the MEI should mean and write it into the rule; the point is to be explicit, since real data almost never land exactly on it.
