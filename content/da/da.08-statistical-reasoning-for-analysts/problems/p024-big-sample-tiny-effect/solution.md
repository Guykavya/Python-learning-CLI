## Approach

Move from "is there an effect?" to "how big is it, and does it pay?".

## Code

Nothing to run. A back-of-envelope: 0.02 points on 100,000 monthly checkouts is 20 extra orders; at 500 rupees each that is 10,000 rupees a month, to be compared with the cost of two engineering weeks and the slower page.

## Another way

Set the minimum effect of interest before the test (problem 23); an interval of about 0.01 to 0.03 points against an MEI of 0.5 points is "not worth it".

## Pitfall to remember

Significant does not mean important.
