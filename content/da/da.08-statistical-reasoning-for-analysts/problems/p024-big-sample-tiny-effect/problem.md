# Big sample, tiny effect

Chapter section 6. An experiment on the checkout page had 2,000,000 customers in each group. The new page converted 10.02% against
10.00%, a lift of **0.02 percentage points**, with `p < 0.001`. The manager emails: "It is significant! Ship it everywhere."
The change costs two engineering weeks and slightly slows the page.

In `answer.md`, in about 150 to 250 words, write the reply you would send:

1. Say what the p-value tells and what it does not.
2. Say what you would report instead (and why the size matters first).
3. Say what decision-relevant information is still missing, and what you recommend.

## Example

A start: "The test shows that the lift is very unlikely to be exactly zero. With two million customers per group almost any difference becomes
detectable. What matters is how big it is..."
