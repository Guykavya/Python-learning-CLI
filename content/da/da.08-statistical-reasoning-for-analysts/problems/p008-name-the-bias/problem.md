# Name the bias

Chapter section 3. Each scenario hides one kind of sampling or measurement bias. In `answer.md`, for each of the six scenarios,
write the **name of the bias** (choose from: selection bias, non-response bias, survivorship bias, voluntary-response bias,
measurement bias, convenience sampling) and **one sentence** saying why.

1. To estimate average income in a city, a student interviews people at the entrance of a luxury mall.
2. A company mails a 40-question survey to 5,000 customers; the 3% who answer are mostly very happy or very angry.
3. A fund advertises "our funds beat the market by 4% a year", counting only the funds that still exist today.
4. A news site posts "Do you support the new rule?" and reports the percentage of the 12,000 people who clicked a button.
5. A scale in a clinic always reads 2 kg too heavy; a study uses it to measure weight loss.
6. A company studies "customer satisfaction" by sampling only customers who completed a purchase.

## Example

"0. A poll by phone calls only landlines: **selection bias**, because people without a landline have no chance to be in the sample."
