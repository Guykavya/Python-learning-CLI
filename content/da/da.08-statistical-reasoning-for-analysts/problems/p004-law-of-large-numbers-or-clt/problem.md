# Law of large numbers or CLT?

Chapter section 2. Two famous results are often mixed up. A colleague says: *"Our sample has 5,000 customers, so by the central
limit theorem our data are normal."*

In `answer.md`, in about 120 to 200 words:

1. State the **law of large numbers** in one sentence and the **central limit theorem** in one sentence, and say how they differ.
2. Explain what is wrong with the colleague's sentence.
3. Say what the CLT does *not* depend on (population size) and what makes it work slowly (very skewed data).

## Example

A start: "The law of large numbers says that the sample average gets closer to the true mean as n grows. The CLT says something about the
*shape* of ..."
