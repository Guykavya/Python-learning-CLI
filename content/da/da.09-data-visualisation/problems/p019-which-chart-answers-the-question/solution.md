## Approach

Work from the question word: "which is most" is a comparison, "how did it change" is time, "how are they spread" is a distribution, "does A go with B" is a relationship, "what share" is part-to-whole, "how satisfied" on a scale is a balance around a neutral middle, "every pair" is a matrix, and "how sure" is uncertainty.
