# Honest or misleading?

Chapter section 6.10. Five charts were sent to you for review. For each one, say **what the reader will be led to believe** and **what you would change**.
Use the honesty checklist of section 6.10 (baseline, lie factor, scale, window, gaps, encoding, colour, labels, title).

1. **A bar chart** of 2023 and 2024 revenue (2.108 and 2.344 million) whose y axis runs from 2.0 to 2.4.
2. **A dual-axis line chart**: revenue of Airport (left axis 0 to 60) and Harbour (right axis 10 to 55); the lines cross twice.
3. **A pie chart** of revenue by store, nine slices, with similar sizes, in rainbow colours.
4. **A 3D bar chart** of sales by category, rotated, where the front bars hide the back ones.
5. **A line chart titled "Sales exploded"** that shows only the last 3 months of a two-year series.

In `answer.md`, give two or three sentences per chart (what it makes the reader believe, the fix). About 250 to 350 words in total.

## Example

"0. A bar chart of customers by city with the y axis starting at 4,000. Reader's belief: the second city has several times more customers. Fix: start the axis at 0 and write the values on the bars."
