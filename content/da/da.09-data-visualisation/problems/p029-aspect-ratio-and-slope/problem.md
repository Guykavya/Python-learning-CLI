# Aspect ratio and slope

Chapter section 6.6. The same two years of monthly revenue are drawn twice: once in a wide, flat figure of **10 x 2.5 inches** (the line rises at about 3 degrees),
once in a tall, narrow figure of **3 x 6 inches** (about 22 degrees). Both y axes start at 0 and both charts are "correct".

In `answer.md`, in about 150 to 250 words:

1. What changes between the two pictures, and what does **not** change?
2. Which message does each shape send to a reader who glances at it?
3. How would you choose the shape of a chart so that it does not push the reader one way?
4. What would you do when two charts will be compared side by side?

## Example

A start: "The data and the axis limits are identical. What changes is the angle of the line, which the eye reads as the speed of growth..."
