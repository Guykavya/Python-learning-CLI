## Hint 1

Think about what happens when something about a **product**, a **customer** or an **order** changes or is counted. For each of the five points ask: in the flat table, how many rows does this touch, and could the answer be wrong?

## Hint 2

The freight of an order with 4 lines appears on 4 rows. What is the sum of the column? And a product that did not sell in a week: does it have a row for that week?
