# Rewrite the vague finding

Chapter sections 1.2 and 6.1. An analyst sent this paragraph to the head of a food-delivery app:

> "Some observations about engagement: it appears to be somewhat down recently, possibly related to the app update. We should monitor the situation and do further analysis."

Their own notes contain the numbers: weekly active users fell from **182,000** (average of the 6 weeks before the update on 3 March) to **161,000** (the 4 weeks after);
the share of sessions that crashed rose from **0.4%** to **3.1%**, almost all on Android 12; users who saw a crash were **2.4 times** as likely to be inactive the next week
(95% CI 2.1 to 2.8). The mobile team can ship a fix in about two weeks.

In `answer.md`, rewrite it as a **headline**, a **recommendation** (action, owner, date), an **impact as a range** in business units, and **one limitation**.
Then say in two sentences what was wrong with the original. About 120 to 200 words.

## Example

A start: "Weekly active users fell from 182,000 to 161,000 after the 3 March app update, mainly because crashes on Android 12 rose from 0.4% to 3.1% of sessions."
