# What makes a finding actionable?

Chapter sections 1.2 to 1.4. A new analyst asks you: "Why do I have to follow that template? Isn't a clear chart enough? And why say what is *wrong* with my own analysis?"

In `answer.md`, explain to them in about 200 to 300 words:

1. why the answer goes **first** (and the path you took goes last);
2. why a recommendation needs an **owner** and a **date**;
3. why the impact is a **range** in business units, not a single number or a statistic;
4. why a finding states its **confidence** and its **limitations** yourself;
5. what single question tells you whether a finding is actionable.

## Example

A start: "A finding exists to change a decision. The reader is busy and may stop after two lines, so the first line must already be the answer..."
