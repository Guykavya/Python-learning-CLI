# Which hypothesis first?

Chapter section 4.3. A grocery app's weekly orders per customer fell from 1.9 to 1.6 in June. The team lists five hypotheses:

1. A competitor opened in two of our eight cities in late May.
2. The new checkout page (released 3 June to all users) is harder to use.
3. It is the start of the summer holidays: people order less every June.
4. We raised the minimum order value from 300 to 400 rupees on 1 June.
5. The mobile app crashed for two days in mid-June.

In `answer.md`:

- order the five by **what you would check first**, using two criteria: how **cheap and quick** the check is, and how **big** an effect it could explain;
- for each, name the **data or comparison** that would test it (what you would expect to see if it were true, and what would refute it);
- point out which hypotheses come with a natural **comparison group**.

About 200 to 300 words.

## Example

"First: 3 (season). Cheap: compare June with June of last year, which we already have. If orders fell just as much last June, most of the drop is seasonal..."
