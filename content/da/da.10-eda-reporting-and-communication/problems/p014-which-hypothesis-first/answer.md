## Model answer

**2nd: the crash (5).** Also cheap: plot daily orders. A two-day crash shows as a dip and a recovery; it can explain a short drop, not a lower level for the whole month. Refuted if orders were already lower before the crash.

**3rd: the minimum order value (4).** It can explain a lot, and it has a natural comparison group: customers whose usual basket was already above 400 rupees were not affected. If true, the drop is concentrated in customers with small baskets; refuted if both groups fell alike.

**4th: the competitor (1).** Also a comparison group: the six cities without the competitor. If true, the drop is larger in the two cities (a difference in differences); refuted if all eight cities fell alike.

**5th: the checkout page (2).** It went to everyone on 3 June, close to the other changes, so there is no clean comparison group in our data. Check the checkout funnel (share of started baskets completed) before and after 3 June. Refuted if completion did not change. The next release should be A/B-tested.
