- **`qty` as `int64` with `astype`.** A missing value makes this raise (`cannot convert NA to integer`); `float64` silently turns 2 into 2.0. Use `Int64`.

- **Filling the gaps with 0.** "No quantity" is not 0 units: keep it missing.
