## Hint 1

Ask the same question three times: "what does the chance of being missing depend on?" Is the answer nothing, a column
that is in the table, or the very value that is not in the table? For each case, who ends up in the rows that remain,
and does the remaining group look like the whole group?

## Hint 2

```text
1. MCAR: nothing | MAR: a visible column | MNAR: the missing value itself
2. survey: MCAR = lost page; MAR = younger people skip income; MNAR = high earners hide income
3. mean of the answered: MCAR unbiased | MAR biased but repairable with visible columns | MNAR biased, not repairable
4. data alone: can see signs of MAR (profile, gap); cannot prove MCAR; cannot separate MAR from MNAR
```
Write a short paragraph for each numbered question.
