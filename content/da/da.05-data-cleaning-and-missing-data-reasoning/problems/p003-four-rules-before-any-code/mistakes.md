- **Saying only "he should be more careful".** The rules are about *process*: raw data kept, a script, a log. Care does
  not make a hand edit reproducible.
- **Naming rules without saying what goes wrong.** "Rule 2" alone is not an explanation; say that the effect on the
  averages cannot be reported.
- **"Deleting is fine if the rows are really bad."** Maybe, but only with a reason you can write down, and a flag first.
  Rows that "looked wrong" to one person are not a rule.
- **Treating "the averages look fine" as the check.** Looking plausible is not a comparison with anything known.
