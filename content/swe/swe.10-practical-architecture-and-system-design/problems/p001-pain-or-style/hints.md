## Hint 1

For each complaint ask the three questions of section 1.3: (1) what concrete pain have I **seen**? (2) how will I know it is fixed? (3) what does the layer cost? If the first answer is "nobody has seen it, it just feels wrong", write `style`.

## Hint 2

When it is a pain, find the sentence of the ladder that matches its **cause**, not its symptom: a rule copied into several routes is a service-layer pain; tests that need a database because rules and SQL are mixed is a repository pain; half a change left behind is a transaction pain; a reaction that must not be lost is an events pain; people and load that cannot be separated is a services pain; rules scattered around the data they protect is a domain pain.
