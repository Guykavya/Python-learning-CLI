## Hint 1

For each feature ask the chapter's question: "what is worse for the user, **an error** or **a wrong or old answer**?" Money and business limits (a rule that must never be broken) want C; things that converge later (feeds, counters, presence) can take A. Then say in plain words what appears on the screen.

## Hint 2

For part 2, think of two people changing the same field on the two sides of a partition, and of what "the newest timestamp wins" does to the loser. For part 3, remember that the C in CAP is about a partition; PACELC asks what you trade when the network is fine (latency versus consistency).
